[PAUSE 1200] Lights of Earth

[Narrator]
Forty-three minutes and eighteen seconds.

[Narrator]

[Narrator]

[Narrator]

[PAUSE 1200] Chapter 3. The Discrepancy

[Narrator]
Nearing the mouth, thrust cuts for the second time. The approach has to be precise, and precision means coasting, and coasting means weightlessness. Loose hair lifts. Pens drift.

[Narrator]
Zao's audit, which she has quietly extended past the point where anyone asked her to stop, flags a discrepancy in propellant.

[Narrator]
It is small. It is also exact. Across the whole accumulated local burn, the Ardor has consumed a measurable fraction more than a one-gravity profile should have required. The number is only meaningful now, at the end; earlier it would have hidden inside metering tolerance and loading uncertainty and looked like nothing at all.

[Narrator]
The ship has held her schedule perfectly. She simply spent more to do it.

[Narrator]
Zao rechecks, because she always rechecks, and because a man on this ship said I wish they were right in a voice she has not been able to put down since.

[Narrator]
There are three ways to lose propellant you did not plan to lose. A leak. An inefficient burn at the wrong mixture ratio. Or mass you did not know you were carrying.

[Narrator]
She works the second one first, because it is testable. The torch runs deuterium and helium three, a comparatively clean cycle but not a clean one: deuterium–deuterium side reactions throw fast neutrons, and one branch makes tritium, which fuses with deuterium and throws harder ones. Starve the helium three feed and the plasma goes deuterium-rich, and the engine burns extra fuel holding the thrust it was told to hold, and the neutron count climbs.

[Narrator]
Which is why, when her engineering dosimeter starts reading a weak but genuine neutron excess above the reactor's low-power baseline, she does not immediately know what she has found. The neutrons have come through shielding. The instrument on her suit is not a spectrometer. An off-ratio burn and a small shielded source look the same to it.

[Narrator]
No fuel alarm is showing. Zao has never once treated the absence of an alarm as proof that nothing is wrong.

[Narrator]
She goes aft, into the outer reactor service bay, and follows the reading as it strengthens — away from the accessible fuel panels, and toward the inner shielding vault.
