# Festival Cut — Restructured Outline (working draft, English)

## Note on this document

This is a **proposal**, not an edit to `outline-light-delay-festival.json`. Nothing in the
actual production data has been touched. Purpose: lock down the causal structure of the
restructured story — the actual job of an outline, per the existing workflow — before anyone
writes a line of dialogue. Once this is approved, the next step is the script; after that, the
next step (name still pending — "animatic" isn't quite right for whatever your pipeline actually
calls that stage).

**Why the story changed, in one paragraph:** Harlan's plan is no longer "corrupt the greeting
laser." He has smuggled a nuclear device aboard, intending to detonate it at the Velari
station, sacrificing the whole crew including himself — motivated by a belief that dependency
on a civilization this far ahead is an existential risk not worth the alternative of prevention.
This required cutting the diplomatic core as a location (it no longer has a load-bearing plot
job), rebuilding Zao's discovery and the entire investigation arc around a hidden physical
device instead of corrupted software, hardening Harlan's cover-up (he *breaks* cameras now, not
just erases footage), and changing Sorell's arc from "detained then cleared" to "jailed until
Harlan is exposed" — which now also removes the one person capable of running first contact from
play until the climax, raising the stakes beyond just the bomb.

**What's unaffected and doesn't need re-litigating:** the throat/return-window mechanics (single
energy cycle, matched pair of ~60–90s openings, missing the return window means stranded but not
destroyed), the light-delay message-aimed-at-the-ship's-own-future-position mechanism, the
off-screen murder, the time-jump structure, and the six-character cast (Zao, Voss, Harlan, Elin,
Sorell, Okoye — Cael stays cut).

**v3 status:** not discarded. Its general dialogue technique (the Zao muttering additions, the
coverage-balance principle, the countdown-visibility idea) still applies. Its Sequence D/E
content specific to the diplomatic core and the corrupted-laser payload is superseded by this
document and shouldn't be carried into the script pass.

## New location: Reactor Shielding Bay

Replaces the diplomatic core as the film's central interior location — where the device is
hidden, where the murder happens, where the climax plays out. Sits aft, in the
engineering/reactor zone, behind the reactor's own radiation shielding — which is why the device
went undetected for as long as it did: the reactor is the most actively monitored system on the
ship, but the shielding around it is comparatively inert and unlikely to draw scrutiny on its
own. That asymmetry is worth keeping explicit rather than leaving implied. Open to a better name
than "Reactor Shielding Bay" if one occurs to you — flagged below.
