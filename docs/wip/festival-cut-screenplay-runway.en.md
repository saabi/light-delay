## Dialogue lanes for the screenplay pass

Dialogue must perform an action, expose character under pressure, or connect evidence that images alone cannot make clear. No line should merely translate a display or lecture the audience.

### Preserve from the master

- Harlan and Sorell’s opposed opening interpretations, followed by Voss’s misreading and Zao’s “That wasn’t hope.”
- Voss’s trust in Zao’s second check and the transit exchange about the Ardor, the primer, silence, and possible misreading.
- Zao’s interrupted wireless warning. Her recorded warning remains inaudible until the reception flashback.
- Harlan asking who received the burst, making the mistaken Earth inference, sighing, and later lamenting the loss of the witness he imagined would vindicate him.
- Harlan’s accusation of Sorell and lie about her supposed wish to delay the mission.
- Elin rejecting the claim that Zao simply missed and separating hypotheses from conclusions.
- Harlan’s final plea, Elin’s “It isn’t disarmed” limit, Sorell’s primer line, Voss’s apology, “They’re sending someone,” and “You made it in time.”

### Add only where silence would hide reasoning or emotion

- **F01:** short preflight shorthand between Zao and Elin, ending with Zao choosing to check once more.
- **F03:** Zao may quietly distinguish a clean commanded burn from unexplained mass, then react when the neutron count strengthens. The line must accompany measurement, not announce the plot.
- **F04:** Zao may name the three failed internal paths while testing them. Her elimination of Proxima, Earth, the pilot throat, and a Velari message should be fragmented thought in action; she stops speaking when she discovers the forward interception.
- **F06:** Sorell calls Harlan while descending, then calls the bridge and attempts aid beside Zao. Her speech should become less controlled as no one answers.
- **F07:** Elin uses terse provisional language: normal mixture, no leak, excess mass, separate neutron source, unresolved lock, reconstructed vector. She never reads full interface labels aloud.
- **F08:** Voss’s commands make simultaneous actions legible: abort, hold Harlan, authenticate, release Sorell, locate the command break, send Elin aft. Sorell tracks time and rejection without recapping the evidence.
- **F09:** pursuit dialogue is limited to locked-hatch callouts, remaining time, and the imminent thrust cutoff. Harlan does not narrate his sabotage in the shaft.

## Intentional silence

- Zao’s first recording, so its content and recipient remain deferred.
- The instant before Harlan attacks and the black frame before the fatal impact.
- The throat crossing juxtaposed with the murder’s aftermath.
- The station’s pause before answering and the Velari’s first attentive luminous response.

## Gate before screenplay authoring

Read the Festival outline alone from beginning to end. If a cause, decision, false assumption, or consequence requires this document to make sense, repair the outline first. Only after editorial approval should the empty `ScriptFile` receive scenes, beats or cues.
