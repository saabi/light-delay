## Relay geometry

Illustrative blocking compatible with the distances, not final ephemerides:

- Jupiter→interception: 167.8 AU = approximately 23 h 15 min 33 s.
- Jupiter→Earth: 43 min 18 s, approximately 5.206 AU.
- Earth→interception: approximately 23 h 58 min, about 172.90 AU.
- Relay via Earth: approximately 24 h 41 min 18 s, even with instantaneous retransmission.
- Reference encounter: approximately 23 h 28 min after transmission.

The three sides satisfy the triangle inequality: Earth lies back toward the remote
destination's opposite direction. The Earth route misses the deadline by over an hour.
This is not twice 43 minutes: the second leg reaches the remote destination, not Jupiter.
A reply to the local mouth also fails: the throat closed before the warning reached Earth.
The 17.4-million-kilometre remote leg with symmetric 1 g acceleration/braking takes
approximately 23 h 24 min; 23 h 25 min and three minutes of clearance are editorial roundings
with room for maneuvers, not an exact orbital solution.
