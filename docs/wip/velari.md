## Velari — Unified species description

*Proposed design. Latter notes supersede earlier “glass octopus” shorthand where they conflict.*

### Environment and survival constraints

They are adapted to **microgravity, not to vacuum**. Their vessels hold a pressurized atmosphere while providing essentially no gravity.

The rule: **they require pressure, a suitable breathing atmosphere, and controlled humidity. Outside habitats they need environmental protection—fitted gear or an enclosed vehicle.** Vacuum exposure is rapidly life-threatening for an organism this large and active: not instantaneous, but it ends activity and communication within moments. Transparency does not mean permeable or structurally weak skin.

### Appearance and anatomy

The Velari are large, non-humanoid beings. Early design used a “glass octopus” as a starting point; the locked form keeps a translucent mantle and branching limbs but **does not reproduce terrestrial octopus anatomy**.

- An individual spans roughly **2.5–3 m** in conversational posture, with substantially greater reach when limbs unfold.
- An elongated, **selectively translucent mantle** is the principal body. Softly opalescent internals show through clear tissue; silver-blue, pearl, and violet pigmentation add depth. There is no upright torso, separate neck, or walking stance. Apparent “height” is a chosen orientation relative to a companion, not a fixed relation to a floor.
- **Eight flexible limbs** gather, extend, and curl in coordinated arrangements. **No suckers** and no repetitive circular attachment structures. Limb tips restore **fine, articulated branches**. Broader, controlled limb arrangements replace ubiquitous C-curls.
- **Branching tissue is continuous with the mantle:** faint neural branching extends into the limb roots and diminishes outward, so the communication organ does not look grafted onto an unrelated body. Branching can remain **faintly visible when inactive**; **emitted light** marks deliberate expression—anatomy versus speech.
- Attention is not a humanlike face. Replace a single prominent eye with **several small, irregularly distributed sensory elements**. Gaze is conveyed through **orientation, stillness, and presentation of the mantle**.

### Physiology

They are acclimated to **pressurized microgravity habitats**. They maintain internal fluids behind resilient, moisture-retaining tissues and breathe through protected respiratory structures. Exact atmospheric composition is unspecified and need not be safe for humans.

An aquatic evolutionary ancestry is a plausible origin story only; living in a gaseous habitat implies adaptations beyond “remove gravity from a water animal.” They **cannot routinely inhabit open vacuum** without fitted environmental gear or enclosed vehicles.

### Time sense and lifespan

Individuals are **long-lived but not immortal**—lifespans on the order of several centuries. The throat is older than any living Velari. The individual in the environmental sphere is not a builder; it is an **inheritor of a commitment** made by others, kept across generations as an obligation rather than a temperament. Their patience is a discipline, not a mood.

They experience conversation as **parallel and unhurried**. A pause is a deliberate interval, not a lapse; an individual can hold attention on one thing for periods that would exhaust a human. This is why silence from them reads as a choice, and why they do not fill it.

They do not experience the millennia of waiting personally. What the one in the sphere brings to the meeting is not the memory of building the door but the weight of being the one present when it finally opened.

### Movement

Limbs are manipulators, anchors, and instruments of expression. They move by pushing from structure, catching handholds, and managing momentum. They do not “swim” through thin air. At rest, limbs form a loose controlled arrangement; at work, some anchor while others manipulate. Elegance comes from precise, economical coordination.

Vessel architecture matches three-dimensional bodies: anchoring points, multidirectional work surfaces, and generous clearances replace floors, chairs, and fixed forward stations.

### Luminous communication

Within the mantle lies a specialized **three-dimensional network of light-emitting neurons**—a **voluntarily controlled communication organ**, not involuntary thought-display.

Meaning is encoded in **spatial arrangement, timing, intensity, and wavelength**. Most of the network stays quiet while selected regions participate. A greeting looks like coordinated neurons firing in living glass—not glowing text, holograms, or corona discharges around the body. Other Velari read these patterns as structured language. Communication needs **visibility and attention**; it is neither telepathy nor open access to another mind.

The mantle is the **dominant display**; fainter activity can extend into limb roots.

### Attention, speech, and expression (three interwoven layers)

Attention should be **visible in how the light organizes**, without exposing private thoughts.

1. **Attention** — When a Velari focuses on someone, luminous branches nearer that person become more finely structured and coordinated: patterns converge, hold, and respond, while quieter activity continues elsewhere. A deliberate acknowledgment: *I am attending to you.*
2. **Speech** — The structured, high-bandwidth sequences they choose to transmit (propositions with relationships, assumptions, alternatives, uncertainties in parallel—not only linear sentences).
3. **Expression** — Color, rhythm, and emphasis: the artistry of delivery.

**Palette** extends beyond teal: saturated violet, electric blue, emerald, magenta, occasional amber or coral. These need not map to fixed human emotions.

**Intensity and subtlety coexist:** a brilliant sweep can carry an intricate overlay of fine pulses, gradients, and branching relationships—not a washed-out bloom. Different regions can articulate simultaneous parts of one expression. The feel should be **living marine iridescence + spatial language**, not a rainbow light show.

High bandwidth does not remove misunderstanding, disagreement, or deception. Aesthetic culture lives inside communication itself: rhythm, spacing, transitions, and relationships among patterns can be beautiful before a human understands the structure. Refinement shows as controlled movement, subtle pigmentation, craftsmanship, and deliberate luminous speech—not necessarily jewelry or human luxury.

### Technology and first contact with humans

Optical interfaces extend the biological mantle language. Human AI (e.g. Sorell’s) unfolds simultaneous relationships into sequential human-readable form: it can recover practical meaning while missing nuance or artistry at first.

**What they know about humans.** A great deal about the ancestors, almost nothing about the descendants. The Velari built the throat after **observing primitive humans** and recorded everything they observed: body form, locomotion, tool use, fire, sound-based communication, group behavior, the environments humans lived in. Those records survive and the individual in the sphere has studied them. The primer was therefore not written for an unknown reader—it was written for a reader they had watched, thousands of years before it could read.

What the records cannot tell them is what happened since. The throat’s mechanisms reported only that the mouth had been found and that its finders could read the primer. They do not know modern human technology, language, social structure, or numbers; they cannot be certain the finders are the same lineage they observed, though they expect the same body plan. Before the Ardor’s hull greeting, no Velari has received a human message. The individual in the sphere expects a human inside the suit and is right, but has never seen one move in a pressure suit, in microgravity, or on a tether.

**The ignorance is asymmetrical**: the Velari know far more about humans than humans know about them, and they know that humans do not know they were watched. Stage the meeting accordingly. The restraint on display is that of the better-informed party choosing not to act on its advantage—not of two unknowns meeting on equal footing.

**What they sense at contact (authorial fact; disclosed to the reader, if at all, only after the meeting—never to the crew within the story).** Their instruments read the Ardor’s mass and the fission primary at contact range. They know the ship carries a weapon; they do not know whether it is aimed, timed, or contested aboard. They could not have known this before the Ardor emerged from the far mouth. **They come anyway.** They do not intervene, warn, or withdraw, because intervention would be the imposition they have refused for thousands of years. Restraint, here, means knowing and approaching. The humans never learn this.

**Greeting staging (visual / novel / film):**

- The Velari gathers limbs to avoid overwhelming the human’s space, orients its sensory region, and presents the mantle.
- Some limbs may extend beyond frame; architecture may partly obscure the silhouette—**suggest scale without hiding the mantle’s essential detail**.
- Illumination of the encounter: neural light reveals the human’s visor, nearby structure, and selected portions of the Velari; retain enough reflected light to establish solid substance.
- Defining beat: **the human waits in darkness; the Velari begins to speak, and the encounter becomes visible.**

**Impression to preserve:** an unfamiliar intelligence making a deliberate effort to be understood—and to make that encounter beautiful.

### Open / unspecified (intentionally)

- Exact atmospheric mix (need not match human air)
- Whether ancestry was aquatic (proposed only)
- Fixed emotion↔color codes (none)
- Exact lifespan figure and social structure of the station (several centuries; one individual sent—who decides is unspecified)
- Whether the coda discloses what they sensed (authorial fact above; disclosure is a novel-level choice)
- Whether the primer disclosed the ancient observation period (lean: it did not—humans learn they were watched only if the coda or the meeting says so; if it did, the Prologue protests would already be arguing about it)
