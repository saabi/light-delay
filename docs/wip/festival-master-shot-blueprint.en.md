# Light Delay — Master-derived Festival Cut shot blueprint

*Editorial blueprint. English source. Derived from Festival outline revision 1 / screenplay 0.2.0-wip. This is not a production shot list: the ScriptFile `shots` and `takes` arrays remain empty.*

## Rules

- Reserved shot IDs are stable planning handles, not implemented `Shot` records.
- Every video candidate is eight seconds or shorter. Longer dramatic moments are divided into adjacent candidates.
- Displays are in English. Spanish display replacement is deferred to post-production planning.
- The transmission’s destination, mechanism, and recording content remain visually and audibly withheld until F08.
- Harlan does not touch flight controls before Zao’s murder.
- Gravity events are distinct: periapsis turnover, precision crossing cutoff, mid-leg braking turnover, and final braking cutoff.
- Existing images may be evaluated later as explicitly marked placeholders; this blueprint assigns no image or generation asset.
