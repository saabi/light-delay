# Light Delay — Festival Cut

*Audience narrative. Festival outline Revision 1, derived from master outline Revision 19. English source.*

## Chapter 1. Earth and Departure

<!-- audience-source-step: festival-master:story-01 -->

Earth reaches Proxima Station forty-three minutes and eighteen seconds late. Every demonstration the *Celestial Ardor* crew watches is already history.

One crowd welcomes the throat beside Jupiter. Another sees an alien door installed inside humanity's home. Rylen Harlan watches the hopeful faces without sharing their confidence.

<!-- audience-dialogue-id: audience:dialogue:festival-f01-harlan-wish -->
> **Rylen Harlan:** "I wish they were right."

Sorell answers that the Velari supplied a primer and waited; humanity can answer, listen, and judge. Voss mistakes Harlan's dread for hope and Sorell's discipline for reluctance.

Only after Sorell has spoken does Zao recognize the error. She waits until Voss is alone.

<!-- audience-dialogue-id: audience:dialogue:festival-f01-zao-not-hope -->
> **Zao:** "That wasn't hope."

Voss smiles as if she has found a harmless fault in his reading of the room.

<!-- audience-dialogue-id: audience:dialogue:festival-f01-voss-faults -->
> **Elias Voss:** "You find faults for a living. That doesn't make everyone a fault."

<!-- audience-source-step: festival-master:story-02 -->

The schedule arrived too late to bring diplomats from Earth. The local six-person crew must take the research ship near Jupiter. Loading becomes a race. Voss calls Harlan Commander; hazardous stores and preparation answer to him.

Below, Zao and Elin Rao finish the post-loading check. Elin reaches the last item. Zao returns to the first.

<!-- audience-dialogue-id: audience:dialogue:festival-f01-elin-ready -->
> **Elin Rao:** "The manifest balances. We're ready."

<!-- audience-dialogue-id: audience:dialogue:festival-f01-zao-again -->
> **Zao:** "From this direction. Give me one pass from the other."

Voss grants the minute. Zao remains with the records.

## Chapter 2. Transit

<!-- audience-source-step: festival-master:story-03 -->

The *Ardor* accelerates toward the Jupiter mouth. A joke about its name and shape becomes a serious question: how should humanity greet an intelligence that has never answered?

Sorell shows the luminous primer. AI can arrange its patterns, she says, but cannot remove uncertainty.

<!-- audience-dialogue-id: audience:dialogue:festival-f02-harlan-misread -->
> **Rylen Harlan:** "And what if we're the ones being read wrong?"

At periapsis the engines cut. The ship enters microgravity for the first time, turns against Jupiter, then resumes thrust. The outbound throat will close behind them; there is no immediate way home.

## Chapter 3. Discovery

<!-- audience-source-step: festival-master:story-04 -->

Near the mouth, thrust stops for precision crossing. The *Ardor* enters microgravity for the second time.

Zao is still auditing. The propellant excess is small but exact: maintaining one gravity consumed more fuel than the declared mass required. No recorded inefficiency explains it.

<!-- audience-dialogue-id: audience:dialogue:festival-f03-zao-mass -->
> **Zao:** "The burn is clean. So what were we carrying?"

Her dosimeter finds a weak neutron excess strengthening toward the shielded vault. It might still be a fusion anomaly, but it gives her a direction.

<!-- audience-source-step: festival-master:story-05 -->

Harlan emerges after an apparently legitimate vault inspection. He has not touched the flight controls, and nothing visible proves wrongdoing. He now knows where Zao's instruments are leading her.

The unsecured vault contains a multi-megaton Proxima geophysical impulse package timed for first contact. No ordinary warning can return before detonation.

## Chapter 4. Isolation

<!-- audience-source-step: festival-master:story-06 -->

Harlan climbs the service shaft to report a legitimate duty. He emerges behind a bridge crew facing their displays just as Zao calls.

<!-- audience-dialogue-id: audience:dialogue:festival-f04-zao-warning -->
> **Zao:** "I have found a grave attempt to sabotage the mission—"

Harlan activates his prepared wrist jammer. Zao's voice vanishes. Before anyone turns, he slips into the shaft, disconnects her deck's wired communications and cameras, and descends.

Voss assigns Elin to the outage and keeps security chief Dara Okoye on the bridge. He orders Sorell to find Zao, locate Harlan, and not go alone. No one answers. With the warning unfinished, she takes the slower helical stairs anyway.

<!-- audience-source-step: festival-master:story-07 -->

Zao tests every route available to her.

<!-- audience-dialogue-id: audience:dialogue:festival-f04-zao-links -->
> **Zao:** "Radio, hardline, emergency. All three. That's deliberate."

The optical array retains independent local control. Proxima is hidden behind Jupiter. Earth could receive a warning, but not answer before the blast. The throat accepts pilot states, not messages. Without Sorell's primer and a confirmed target, Zao cannot warn the Velari.

<!-- audience-dialogue-id: audience:dialogue:festival-f04-zao-options -->
> **Zao:** "Not back. Not through. Not to them."

Then two clocks on her display align. She stops speaking and calculates. The audience sees only her result: twenty-three hours, fifteen minutes.

## Chapter 5. Warning and Murder

<!-- audience-source-step: festival-master:story-08 -->

Zao records less than a minute into volatile memory, visible but unheard. She names the package, origin, location, yield, protections, signature, and deadline, then signs and sends a narrow burst. Its destination and path remain hidden.

Harlan enters and sees that something was transmitted.

<!-- audience-dialogue-id: audience:dialogue:festival-f05-harlan-recipient -->
> **Rylen Harlan:** "Who did you send that to?"

Zao refuses to answer. With Sorell approaching, Harlan does not reconstruct the vector. Proxima is hidden, the throat does not listen, and Zao cannot speak Velari. He assumes Earth.

<!-- audience-dialogue-id: audience:dialogue:festival-f05-harlan-earth -->
> **Rylen Harlan:** "Earth will know. But their answer cannot reach us before the encounter."

He believes the accusation will arrive too late to stop him but may someday vindicate him. He sighs, sorry that this future witness must die.

<!-- audience-source-step: festival-master:story-09 -->

Harlan closes the distance. The image cuts before the blow. A single thump lands in darkness.

He leaves Zao drifting outside the vault and secures the bomb. Only now, because her warning makes an abort foreseeable, he disconnects bridge flight commands and the remote cutoff locally. The automatic course and telemetry remain unchanged.

As he climbs, the throat opens. The *Ardor* crosses, and the mouth closes. The bridge celebrates without knowing what happened below or how the vanished burst relates to their arrival.

## Chapter 6. Frame-up

<!-- audience-source-step: festival-master:story-10 -->

Sorell reaches the bay by the slower route and finds Zao's body. She catches her, searches for a pulse, and tries to call out.

<!-- audience-dialogue-id: audience:dialogue:festival-f06-sorell-call -->
> **Lian Sorell:** "Bridge, Zao is down. Medical emergency. Does anyone copy?"

Nothing answers. Harlan reaches the communications tray first, reconnects cables and cameras, disables the jammer, and announces a repair. The restored image shows only Sorell holding Zao's bloodied body.

<!-- audience-source-step: festival-master:story-11 -->

Harlan says he passed Zao while completing assigned duties and never saw Sorell. Voss sends him back with Okoye. On arrival, Harlan turns the scene into an accusation.

<!-- audience-dialogue-id: audience:dialogue:festival-f06-harlan-accuse -->
> **Rylen Harlan:** "How could you? Why?"

There is no brig. Sorell is held on the bridge. Blood, an unrecorded descent, and Harlan's claim that she wanted to delay contact are enough for suspicion, not proof. Voss fits them to his earlier misreading. Elin notices Sorell's grief—and Harlan's speed.

<!-- audience-source-step: festival-master:story-12 -->

Beyond the far mouth, thrust returns for the twenty-three-and-a-half-hour station leg. Voss keeps the case open and orders communications, mass, records, and Zao's work reconstructed. Detained, Sorell cannot prepare the greeting.

## Chapter 7. Investigation

<!-- audience-source-step: festival-master:story-13 -->

Harlan says Sorell altered the fusion mix: a deuterium-rich burn could consume more fuel, shed more neutrons, and delay contact. Voss finds it plausible. Elin calls it a hypothesis.

Hours of audit separate the clues. The mixture was normal. Nothing leaked. The engine remained efficient.

<!-- audience-dialogue-id: audience:dialogue:festival-f07-elin-mass -->
> **Elin Rao:** "The engine burned extra fuel because the ship was heavier. The neutrons came from somewhere else."

Harlan's neat explanation accounts for neither fact. The case against Sorell weakens.

<!-- audience-source-step: festival-master:story-14 -->

Midway to the station, the ship enters microgravity for the third time and turns to begin braking, making the missing hours physical.

Elin and Okoye trace neutrons to the vault, whose local lock rejects Voss. Harlan blames Sorell. The data proves hidden mass and a compact source, not yet a bomb. Elin leaves a time-reference diagnostic and returns to the optical logs.

<!-- audience-source-step: festival-master:story-15 -->

Only the ship's attitude and firing angles survive. The vector points to no planet or beacon. Concealing that he saw the burst, Harlan says Zao aimed at Earth and missed.

<!-- audience-dialogue-id: audience:dialogue:festival-f07-elin-no-miss -->
> **Elin Rao:** "Zao didn't miss."

She compares the empty direction with moving trajectories. Alone, Harlan mourns the witness he thought would justify him.

<!-- audience-dialogue-id: audience:dialogue:festival-f07-harlan-saved -->
> **Rylen Harlan:** "Now they'll never know who saved them."

Thirteen minutes before contact, light arrives along the same corridor. The ship itself was the moving receiver.

## Chapter 8. Revelation

<!-- audience-source-step: festival-master:story-16 -->

Elin recognizes Zao's packet format and signed header. Voss opens it at once.

Zao's recording is heard at last. Before crossing, she aimed through ordinary space where the *Ardor* would emerge twenty-three light-hours ahead. The ship jumped beyond its warning; the beam spent the next day catching up.

Zao's message identifies the bomb and its protections. It ends with the danger already outside her door.

<!-- audience-dialogue-id: audience:dialogue:festival-f08-zao-goodbye -->
> **Zao:** "Just in case: goodbye. Harlan's at the door."

<!-- audience-dialogue-id: audience:dialogue:festival-f08-voss-abort -->
> **Elias Voss:** "Abort the approach. Hold Harlan."

The bridge rejects the command. Okoye restrains Harlan.

<!-- audience-source-step: festival-master:story-17 -->

Harlan says voice and image can be fabricated. Elin instead uses Zao's personal signature and timestamp. Her warning agrees with the hidden mass, neutron source, optical vector, Harlan's missing location, accusation, and lie. No clue excludes every accomplice; together they justify isolating him and releasing Sorell.

<!-- audience-source-step: festival-master:story-18 -->

Voss revokes Harlan's authority and releases Sorell: the accusation failed, and the mission needs her. For eight minutes, normal controls cannot interrupt the autonomous plan. Commands arrive but do not reach it. Only the aft console can break the chain.

Three minutes remain. Elin takes the elevator aft. Sorell rebuilds the greeting. Okoye must recover Harlan's wrist device to open the vault.

## Chapter 9. Chase

<!-- audience-source-step: festival-master:story-19 -->

Harlan appears to surrender the device. When Okoye glances away, he drives her into the console and dives into the service shaft, locking it behind him. To stop Elin from diverting the ship or opening the bomb, he must reach her first.

Elin arrives by elevator. Harlan descends the segmented ladder, sealing each access, and attacks before she can finish. Voss and Okoye take the helical stairs. Two locked hatches cost them precious time.

<!-- audience-dialogue-id: audience:dialogue:festival-f09-okoye-time -->
> **Dara Okoye:** "One minute. He's in the bay with her."

<!-- audience-source-step: festival-master:story-20 -->

The automatic plan completes braking. The ship enters microgravity for the fourth time.

The stairs cease to be stairs. Okoye launches through the central opening; Voss follows. They cross fifteen metres in seconds, tether Harlan, and recover his wrist device.

Harlan does not boast. He pleads.

<!-- audience-dialogue-id: audience:dialogue:festival-f09-harlan-answer -->
> **Rylen Harlan:** "You don't know what they are. You don't get to answer for Earth."

He wanted to delay contact and buy humanity time by proving it bites. Voss does not debate while the timer runs.

## Chapter 10. Contact

<!-- audience-source-step: festival-master:story-21 -->

Elin opens the vault with Harlan's wrist device. Zao's warning and the local indicators agree: cutting power or damaging the controller could trigger the weapon.

The diagnostic unit Elin left behind can supply an external time reference. She connects it. The bomb counts four, three, two, one—then its timer jumps upward while the ship's independent clock passes the original deadline.

<!-- audience-dialogue-id: audience:dialogue:festival-f10-elin-delayed -->
> **Elin Rao:** "It isn't disarmed. It just isn't going off here."

The borrowed interval leaves the charge armed but watched and keeps the encounter alive.

<!-- audience-source-step: festival-master:story-22 -->

On the bridge, Sorell finishes the greeting. The AI unfolds the primer's spatial grammar into branching lights across the hull. It is not another beam aimed into darkness.

<!-- audience-dialogue-id: audience:dialogue:festival-f10-sorell-primer -->
> **Lian Sorell:** "They wrote the primer. This is the first time they'll hear us read it back."

Voss apologizes for misreading her, sending her without an escort, and detaining her. Then he authorizes one greeting.

<!-- audience-source-step: festival-master:story-23 -->

Light travels across the *Ardor's* hull. The station waits.

Then its four-kilometre structure answers with an immense luminous grammar the AI can only begin to parse.

<!-- audience-dialogue-id: audience:dialogue:festival-f10-sorell-someone -->
> **Lian Sorell:** "They're sending someone."

A transparent environmental sphere approaches. Sorell goes out alone on a tether. A Velari nearly three metres tall orients its mantle, focuses on her, then extends the human greeting in living light. It does not prove safety; it proves violence was not the only answer.

## Chapter 11. Record

<!-- audience-source-step: festival-master:story-24 -->

Flight controls still need repair. The bomb still needs a watch. Before the return, Voss uses the optical array to send Earth an independent record of the discovery, murder, warning, intervention, and contact.

He attaches Zao's signed message and personnel record. Her photograph remains on the display after the transmission leaves.

<!-- audience-dialogue-id: audience:dialogue:festival-f11-voss-time -->
> **Elias Voss:** "You made it in time."

The new message departs into the same silence she trusted.
